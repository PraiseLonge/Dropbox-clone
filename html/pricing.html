How Dropbox compares:

VS GOOGLE DRIVE

Work across teams, tools, and file types

Google Drive works best for teams fully committed to Google Workspace. Dropbox is platform-agnostic, making it easier to collaborate on Microsoft, Adobe, video, and other professional file types — especially with partners outside your organization and it can be faster for real work at scale (e.g., 71% faster uploads for folders with 10,000 files*).

VS MICROSOFT ONEDRIVE

Clear collaboration — without the complexity

OneDrive spreads work across multiple Microsoft tools, which can make collaboration harder to follow, especially with external partners. Dropbox gives teams a single, clear place to work, with fast syncing and built-in tools for reviewing, sharing, and managing files across devices — including performance advantages like 21x faster uploads of large folders*.

*with 10,000 1 KB files

VS BOX

Powerful collaboration, designed to scale with teams

Box is optimized for highly regulated, large-enterprise environments with extensive configuration needs. Dropbox is designed to scale from small teams to large organizations, combining strong security with fast performance and simpler collaboration — especially when work spans internal teams and external partners — with reported performance wins like 95% faster end-to-end sync across devices*.

*on 25 MB files

*based on a 2023 Principled Technologies study sponsored by Dropbox

Frequently asked questions

Which plan is best for me?

It depends on whether you are an individual, a small team, or a large company. Plus is great for personal use, Standard for teams needing collaboration features, and Advanced for companies requiring top-tier security and admin controls.

We're a very small team (1-2 people). Do we need a team plan?

While you can use Plus individually, team plans like Standard start at 1 person and offer collaborative features like shared team folders and centralized admin controls that make working together much easier.

What is the difference between team folders and personal folders?

Personal folders are private to you. Team folders are shared spaces where multiple team members can access, edit, and manage files together, ensuring everyone has the latest version.

Do shared folders count against my team's storage?

Yes, files stored in shared team folders count against the total storage pool of the team plan.

Can clients upload files without a Dropbox account?

Yes, with File Requests, clients can upload files to a specific folder using a simple link without needing to create a Dropbox account.

Can upload-only users be blocked from seeing other files?

Yes, you can set permissions on file requests to allow uploads without giving view or edit access to the rest of the folder contents.

What do the team free trials include, and what happens when they end?

Free trials give you full access to the team plan features for a limited time. When the trial ends, your account will revert to a free Basic plan unless you choose to purchase a subscription.
